# GAYONGX: WARISAN

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User specified Phaser 3, TypeScript and Vite. Static deployment, no backend or external runtime services.

## Product Purpose

A complete playable Malaysian coastal martial arts vertical slice, aiming at a 5–10 minute first playthrough. Exploration, guided training, three intruders, a final duel and a cinematic ending.

## Brand Commitments

Historical fiction, Malay dialogue, restrained sunset gold, forest green and blue-grey coastal atmosphere. Arif is fictional. Meor is a respectfully portrayed historical inspiration; all dialogue and game events are dramatized. No claim of official association endorsement.

## Operating Context

Assumption authorized by the autonomous brief: desktop/laptop keyboard and mobile landscape touch play. Local npm development and Vercel static build.

## Product Principles

Working complete loop first. Responsive readable combat. Original procedural art. No copyrighted third-party sprites. Honest testing and historical framing.
