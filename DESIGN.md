# Visual direction

Brief-pinned direction: a cinematic coastal dusk, original layered procedural scenery, martial arts silhouettes and quiet editorial titles. Experience mode; the game leads.

Canvas: 1280 × 720, fitted to desktop/laptop and landscape mobile with multi-touch controls. Warm sky reflected in blue-green water; near-black foliage frames the lower corners. Wooden houses on stilts, woven panels, hand-drawn coconut fronds, boats, nets and lanterns establish place. The supplied PSSGM logo appears in the menu, favicon and social card by user request. No military insignia or claim of official endorsement.

Menu: title and three understated navigation rows on the left, environment and posed fighter on the right. Cormorant Garamond title and Manrope UI, bundled locally. Brass #d8b579, parchment #f5ead4, deep green #101b1b, muted red #ad624d. Visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion option, sound toggle. HUD recesses around the world; combat telegraphs use shape, text and color.

All assets are generated in code. Cinematics use letterbox, slow camera travel and crossfades. Hit pauses are brief and driven by gameplay time. Pause freezes simulation and resets held keys on resume.
